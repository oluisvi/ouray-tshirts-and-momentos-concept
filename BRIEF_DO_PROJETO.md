# Ouray T-Shirts and Momentos — project handoff (private)

## Purpose

An independent, mobile-first website concept for Ouray T-Shirts and Momentos, a brick-and-mortar souvenir and apparel shop in Ouray, Colorado. It was prepared after the shop replied to ServAgency outreach. The deliverable includes a multi-page browsing experience and an illustrative concept board with desktop, tablet and mobile presentations.

## Confirmed by the shop's reply

- Facebook and Instagram are its main online channels.
- The shop is brick-and-mortar; its main source of sales is visitors in Ouray.
- It offers shipping in winter months. Destinations, timing, cost and ordering details were not supplied.
- The contact offered to speak with the manager and potentially send selected product photos.

## Facts from public shop/destination sources

- The official public Facebook page is [Ouray Tshirts and momentos](https://www.facebook.com/OurayTshirts/). It describes unique gifts, accessories and souvenirs, and invites visitors to take a photo by the window, tag the shop and ask for a sticker.
- [Visit Ouray's store directory](https://www.visitouray.com/shop) lists 610 Main St, Ouray, CO 81427 and +1 970-325-9868, and links the Facebook page. Other directories contain address variations; validate address/hours with the shop before official launch.
- Current online description and Facebook imagery support broad themes: T-shirts, apparel/warm layers, gifts, accessories and souvenirs. This does not establish what is in stock today.
- No standalone site was confirmed in the lead report or public destination listing.
- An official Instagram profile URL could not be independently confirmed. The concept does not use a guessed or generic Instagram link.

## Design and interaction decisions

- Visual direction: alpine travel postcard meets Main Street shop; forest green, glacier mint, trail-sign gold and warm paper; mountain line art, topo map motifs and compact editorial labels.
- Main route: home / what to find / Ouray guide / visit & contact / concept notes.
- Editorial categories replace fake product SKUs: graphic tees, warm layers/accessories, gifts/souvenirs.
- Main interaction is simple wayfinding into the shop, with direct contact for current selection. Mobile menu supports keyboard/screen readers; motion is restrained and disabled for reduced-motion preference.
- Responsive presentation board appears in `prancha.html` and rendered export in `arte/`.

## Non-goals and unknowns

- No checkout, online-order promise, live inventory, fictitious prices, size claims, product-level stock or reservations.
- No store origin/history or staff names were found/added.
- Exact official Instagram link, opening hours, winter-shipping details, current designs, sizes and photo preferences await manager confirmation.
- The public Facebook photos help make this independent concept tangible. Their public availability is not permission to reuse them commercially; obtain approval or replace them before official adoption.
